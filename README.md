Healthcare UIs have a reputation for being clunky and clinical. I wanted to challenge that, to build something that felt trustworthy and calm without feeling cold. MediCare was about learning how to design for a high-stakes context where users are often stressed. The UI decisions here weren't just aesthetic; they were about communicating reliability.

What made it hard?

Balancing a professional, institutional feel with genuine warmth. Too sterile and it feels like a government form. Too friendly and it loses credibility. Finding that line — and holding it across every section — was the real design challenge.
